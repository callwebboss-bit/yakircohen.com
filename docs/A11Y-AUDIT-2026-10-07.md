# Accessibility audit synthesis: yakircohen.com (origin/main 5e8b3e4a, built at localhost:3230)

**Basis.** I merged the 8 auditors' results and their skeptic rechecks. I ran nothing myself. All numbers are the dimensions' measurements.

**Dimensions:**
- D1 images/alt
- D2 icon controls/names/forms
- D3 focus/skip link
- D4 contrast/reflow
- D5 popups/Escape
- D6 legal research
- D7 axe sweep/misc
- D8 existing statement

**Evidence ids.** "D2/SK-03" means the skeptic-added finding SK-03 in dimension D2. Ids like "D3-01" are original auditor findings. "D3sk-M2", "D4S-01" and "D5/SK-01" follow each dimension's own naming.

**Counts.** 79 findings: 1 blocker, 20 serious, 30 moderate, 24 minor, 4 info.

**Standard.** D6 found that ת"י 5568 (Sep 2023 edition) is WCAG 2.0 plus national changes, not 2.1. SC tagged (2.1) or (2.2) below are therefore best-practice for the Israeli legal yardstick, though still WCAG failures. Per D6 the national changes make 3.1.2 inapplicable and add 2.4.10 at AA.

**Gates.** `audit:seo-diff` fails on any decrease in words, outline entries, links, imagesWithAlt, ldTypes or ldProps, and on any change to title, description, h1 or canonical. It passes only with an owner-approved entry in `scripts/baselines/seo-approved-changes.json`, which no agent may add.
